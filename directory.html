This page needs no JavaScript. Use your browser’s Print command for a PDF or paper copy. Jump to privacy notes.

Android 9+: open Settings → Private DNS → Private DNS provider hostname. Paste one bare hostname, without a scheme, IP address, port or path. Record your previous setting before switching. Official instructions.

These are documented profiles, not live-tested servers. “Not advertised” does not prove a domain is allowed. Filtering is not a VPN and cannot reliably remove same-domain ads. Public presets do not provide your own exceptions; see the interactive guide for personalized services.
